The Process

A journey of creation, from the first hello to the final applause.

I

The Initial Booking

It all begins with a spark. You reach out, we listen. We sit down to understand your vision, cultural nuances, and the exact vibe you wish to create. This is where your rhythm finds its canvas.

II

Blueprint & Curation

Our creative minds get to work. We choreograph, we select the perfect tracks, and we design the flow. A comprehensive artistic proposal is crafted, aligning every beat with your expectations.

III

Rehearsals & Logistics

The gears are in motion. We conduct intensive rehearsals, coordinate with event planners for stage dimensions, and lock down the timeline to ensure absolute perfection.

IV

On The Floor

The day has arrived. Our artists take the floor, translating months of preparation into breathtaking reality. We bring the 'Khanak' so you can soak in the beauty and joy of your moment.
